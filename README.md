# Titanic (1997)

> "Winning that ticket, Rose, was the best thing that ever happened to me... it brought me to you." — **Jack Dawson**

---

## A. Introduction

*Titanic* is a legendary epic romance and disaster film directed, written, co-produced, and co-edited by James Cameron. Released in 1997, the film explores the tragic maiden voyage of the RMS Titanic and blends a stunning historical backdrop with a fictionalized socioeconomic love story. It belongs to the romance and disaster-drama genres.

---

## B. Synopsis

1. An elderly woman named Rose DeWitt Bukater recounts her memories of the ill-fated 1912 maiden voyage of the RMS Titanic.
2. A young, penniless artist named Jack Dawson wins third-class tickets in a poker game and boards the ship just as it departs.
3. Jack rescues a distressed upper-class Rose from a suicide attempt, sparking a forbidden romance across rigid social classes.
4. On the night of April 14, the ship strikes an iceberg, causing catastrophic hull breaches that flood the lower decks.
5. Amidst the chaos and panic of the sinking ship, Jack and Rose fight desperately for survival, leading to a heartbreaking sacrifice in the freezing Atlantic waters.

---

## C. Characters

* **Jack Dawson:** A free-spirited, bohemian artist from Wisconsin who wins a ticket aboard the Titanic in a lucky poker game.
* **Rose DeWitt Bukater:** A 17-year-old upper-class passenger suffocating under societal pressures and an unwanted engagement.
* **Cal Hockley:** Rose’s arrogant, wealthy fiancé who looks down on lower-class passengers.
* **Molly Brown:** A wealthy, socially progressive first-class passenger who treats Jack with kindness and warmth.
* **Captain Edward Smith:** The experienced captain of the RMS Titanic whose career is marred by the tragic disaster.

## D. Personal Review

### Why I Chose This Movie
I chose *Titanic* because it is a cinematic masterpiece that masterfully combines grand historical scale with deeply moving human emotion. 

### Things I Liked About It
* **Groundbreaking Special Effects:** The practical sets mixed with early CGI bring the massive ship and its tragic final hours to terrifyingly realistic life.
* **Iconic Musical Score:** James Horner's timeless composition, featuring the haunting vocals of Celine Dion, elevates every emotional beat.
* **Unforgettable Chemistry:** The dynamic performances by Leonardo DiCaprio and Kate Winslet make the central romance feel completely genuine and enduring.

### Personal Rating
* **Rating:** 10/10

### Recommendation
If you appreciate grand cinematic storytelling, epic historical drama, and powerful romantic tragedies, you should definitely watch this film.
